---
title: Registers — the volume control
order: 10
summary: The alphabet says what may be drawn; registers say how loudly.
---

The five primitives are the **alphabet**; the **registers** are the **volume**. Every composition belongs to exactly one.

- **Identity register** — solid fills, multi-colour, dense (4–16 elements). Exclusively the Signal Mark and identity fields. The loudest voice, therefore the rarest.
- **Functional register** — teal/purple strokes + one or two solid accents, sparse (1–3 elements). Icons, diagrams, UI marks. Drawings *about* work.
- **Atmospheric register** — deep tints (10–50%), never saturated. Backgrounds and drift only.

This is the layer v1.0 lacked: without it, mark, icons, and fields all spoke in solid multi-colour and everything read as a variation of the logo.

**Two laws live here.** *Mark Exclusivity:* no composition may be a 2×2 of four solid primitives except the Signal Mark. *Coloured geometry:* structure strokes are teal or purple — never ink, grey, or white.
