Resolved via specimen comparison (Basel / Engineered / Editorial / Geometric). **Basel won.**

- **Switzer** — display and body. Hero 300, titles 500, wordmark 600. A modern Swiss neo-grotesque; institutional, timeless, zero quirk.
- **Fragment Mono** — labels, kickers, buttons. Single weight (400).
- **Self-host both** in production — GDPR-clean font delivery is itself on-brand for a governance practice.

Space Grotesk, Inter, DM Mono, Archivo: retired.
