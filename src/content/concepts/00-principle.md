> **Everything is generated from a grammar, never hand-orchestrated.**
> If an element needs bespoke art-direction to look right, it is not in the system.

This single rule decided the whole system. It killed the Bauhaus direction (its forms require orchestration) and kept the primitive vocabulary (squares, arcs, circles, leaves, half-discs), which scales by rule.

Two supporting principles:

- **Professional means less, not more.** Public-sector and enterprise audiences read restraint as credibility and expressiveness as noise.
- **Brand mode vs Tool mode is a hard split.** Brand = dark, sparse, cinematic (public site). Tool = light, dense, operational (the Lab). Never mixed on one surface.
